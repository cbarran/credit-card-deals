# Travel Hacking in NYC: Getting the Most Out of Your Credit Card Points

Living in New York City is expensive, but for travel-savvy residents, it’s also one of the best places to leverage credit card points. From dining out to subway commuting, here is how you can maximize your rewards.

## 1. Optimize Your Daily Spend
In NYC, your biggest expenses are likely rent, dining, and transit. Using a card that rewards these categories is essential.

## 2. Choosing the Right Travel Card
- **Sapphire Reserve**: Great for dining and general travel.
- **Amex Gold**: Best for foodies (supermarket and restaurant multipliers).

## 3. Strategic Redemption
Don't just burn your points. Look for transfer partners that offer 2 cents per point or higher.
