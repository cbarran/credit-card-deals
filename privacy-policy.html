Privacy Policy

At TravelCard.info, we believe transparency is the foundation of trust. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, protect, and use information when you interact with our editorial travel guides and credit card comparison tools.

1. Information We Collect

We use standard log files to understand how visitors interact with our site. This includes IP addresses, browser types, Internet Service Providers (ISP), and click patterns. This data is used solely for optimizing your experience and does not identify individuals personally.

2. Advertising & Cookies

TravelCard.info partners with third-party vendors, including Google AdSense. Google uses cookies (such as the DART cookie) to serve relevant advertisements based on your visit to this site and other websites across the internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings.

3. CCPA & GDPR Data Rights

Regardless of your location, we respect your data rights. You have the right to:

  • Request access to any data we have collected.
  • Request that we delete any personal information.
  • Object to the processing of your personal data.

4. Affiliate & Financial Disclosure

TravelCard.info is an independent editorial publication. We may receive compensation from companies whose products are featured on this site. However, our reviews and comparisons are based on objective analysis. Always verify card terms directly with the issuer.
