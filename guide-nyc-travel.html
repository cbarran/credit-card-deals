Travel Hacking in NYC: Maximize Your Points in the City That Never Sleeps

← Back to all guides

New York City is one of the most expensive cities on earth, but for the travel hacker, it’s also a points-earning gold mine. From high-rent payments to daily subway commutes and a world-class dining scene, every dollar spent in the five boroughs can be leveraged for your next flight or hotel stay.

1. The Rent Hack (Bilt Rewards)

For most New Yorkers, rent is the single largest monthly expense. Traditionally, paying rent via credit card incurred heavy processing fees (often 2.9% or more), making it a losing game. However, the Bilt Mastercard changed the landscape by allowing rent payments with zero transaction fees while earning 1 point per dollar.

If you're paying $3,500 in rent, that's 42,000 points a year—essentially a free round-trip domestic flight—just for paying your bills.

2. Navigating Transit with OMNY

With the MTA’s OMNY system, you can now tap-to-pay at every turnstile. This opens up massive opportunities for "Travel" category multipliers. Many premium cards like the Chase Sapphire Reserve or Amex Green code MTA swipes as travel, earning you 3x points on every commute.

3. Dining: The Core of NYC Spend

NYC has over 25,000 restaurants. If you aren't earning at least 4x points on dining, you are leaving money on the table. The American Express Gold Card is the undisputed king here, offering 4x points at restaurants worldwide and U.S. supermarkets.

Pro Tip: Many NYC "delis" and "bodegas" often code as supermarkets, allowing you to maximize spend even on a quick egg-and-cheese sandwich.

4. Strategic Redemption at JFK and EWR

Living in NYC means access to three major airports. To get the most value, avoid the "Travel Portal" trap. Instead, transfer your points directly to airline partners like Virgin Atlantic (for cheap ANA first-class flights from JFK) or Turkish Airlines (for 7,500 point domestic flights).

By treating every NYC transaction as a strategic data point, you can turn your daily survival into a first-class lifestyle.
